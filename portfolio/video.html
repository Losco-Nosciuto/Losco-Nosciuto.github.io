Music video · Crownlake

Golem

Shot at the Castello Visconteo in Vogogna with a Xiaomi 11T Pro and a Xiaomi 13T Pro, in LOG with Blackmagic Camera.

Edited in DaVinci Resolve Studio, relying on its multicam support; only minimal manual correction was needed.

Graded in DaVinci Resolve Studio. The live footage from the castle carries an acid-green tint, a nod to the Saw films. The AI scenes are graded by narrative function: warmer tones for the Golem and its lava, lower contrast and cooler blues for the sad passages, and stronger contrast with motion-driven color for the action.

All AI scenes were generated in multiple passes with Seedance 2.5: references, storyboard and final video. The storyboard was built one slide at a time; character design used T-poses and multiple angles to keep consistency, so the model could start from clean references and need fewer re-prompts.

Golem — Crownlake. Produced by Andrea “Fox” Fossati. All rights reserved.

Music video · Crownlake

Man On the Table

Shot on Xiaomi 11T Pro and 13T Pro in LOG with Blackmagic Camera; entirely live, no AI scenes.

Edited entirely by hand in Premiere Pro, before adopting a multicam workflow: one step at a time.

Heavy smoke and aggressive backlight push the band almost entirely into silhouette, with a blue cast. The blue was accidental: a take with the wrong white balance looked right to everyone, so we graded the whole video around it. A few small mismatches in the drum cam came from a lighting mistake on set; I minimized them with a reference still and manual color tuning.

Man On the Table — Crownlake. Produced by Microstudio. All rights reserved.

MotoVlog · Panoramica Zegna

Rosazza

GoPro Hero 7 Black for the motorcycle footage, helmet-mounted, and a DJI Mini 2 for the aerials over Bielmonte and Rosazza. The aerial sequence starts at 14:41.

Edited entirely in Premiere Pro. Where there is background music, I cut on the beat.

Thematic grading. High contrast for the aggressive music passages; a 1920s silent-comedy treatment for the iconic comic scene — sped-up motion, comic piano, black and white with a damaged-film look. Evocative contrast for the Bielmonte drone sequence, and a desaturated, at times horror-tinged grade for the drone panorama over Rosazza, mirroring the town's dark and mysterious history.

The soundtrack is AI-generated: Djent and EDM for the aggressive scenes, evocative music for the Bielmonte flyover, and cinematic horror with low male choirs in the Phrygian scale for the second flyover, over Rosazza.

Prompt — Rosazza flyover

“Ethereal dark low tone male choirs vocals, Phrygian scale choir, haunting melodies, cinematic horror, A ritual of loss and fear”

MotoVlog · Tuscany

Road Trip to Tuscany, Day 3

GoPro Hero 7 Black for the motorcycle footage, but no drone yet; a few rare clips from a Xiaomi 11T Pro.

Edited in Premiere Pro, cutting on the beat where there is music. I used Google Earth Studio to generate flyovers that give context to cuts jumping between locations kilometers apart — hence Porto Venere in the thumbnail. A few funny memes with iconic clips, here and there.

Saturation and contrast are tuned to deepen the black of the bike, while the grade preserves the chrome reflections and brings out the green of the Ligurian landscape. The arrival in Tuscany is slightly warmer, to give weight to the more arid coastal environment and the harsh heat of those days. There is also a comic scene with a “Funny Circus” cue, while I film my own driving blunders.

MotoVlog · Bons-en-Chablais

Travel to France

GoPro Hero 7 Black for the motorcycle footage, no drone yet; a few clips from a Xiaomi 11T Pro.

Edited entirely in Premiere Pro, with a heavy dose of memes — one of the visual hooks I preferred.

Bons-en-Chablais, fog and wet asphalt. I had just started producing, and the grade was minimalist, often shifting from shot to shot with the mood of the footage itself. I didn't touch the snow clips at the top of the Simplon Pass: the natural grade already evoked the bitter cold of that March 8 of rain and snow. For the scenes with music or shot in good weather, I used a richer grade — saturation and contrast to accentuate color and chroma, and a natural green for the vegetation without making it look artificial.

MSFS trailer · EGLK Blackbushe

Blackbushe V2

Camera work entirely in-sim in Microsoft Flight Simulator. I used a HOTAS, sometimes an Xbox controller; occasionally the camera jittered or jumped, because the control handling and the hardware setup were not ideal.

Edited entirely in Premiere Pro. The camera flies through the virtual airport at ground level, showing it in full, then some shots rise to show it from medium and wide distance. The final fly-up is my favorite: the airport disappears behind the clouds of a typically English day, leaving room for a pseudo-vintage “digital” postcard.

Sepia grade, film grain, damaged-film effect, contrast and vignette.

A swing track generated with Suno, telling the airport's story in a period musical style.

Prompt — EGLK theme

“Big Band and Swing mood, rainy day mood. Melancholic lyrics but music keeps the tone happier even in the saddest moment generating a beautiful contrast without losing the meaning”
