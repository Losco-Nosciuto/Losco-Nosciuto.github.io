The flow, step by step

Each stage has a verifiable output. If a step has no output, it is usually a step that will be redone.

  1. 1

    Ingest, backup and proxy

    4K footage from GoPro and drone, copied and verified; proxies to edit without waiting.

  2. 2

    Rough cut and multicam

    Selection and order first; multicam in Resolve for music videos, where the angle is part of the rhythm.

  3. 3

    Compositing and motion

    After Effects for about half of the effects; graphics and mattes prepared in Photoshop and Illustrator.

  4. 4

    Color

    Resolve, node tree and consistency between shots; export checked on a calibrated screen.

  5. 5

    Audio

    Fairlight: cleanup, levels, fades and loudness aligned with the target platform.

  6. 6

    Export and delivery

    ProRes master, H.264/H.265 for delivery; 2K output, with vertical cuts for social.

On set

I started with MotoVlog: GoPro Hero 7 Black, then a DJI Mini 2. Shooting on the move, audio to recover, and an editing rhythm that has to hold for minutes, not seconds.

With Crownlake the set became controlled: "Man On the Table" was shot entirely in our rehearsal room, with a smoke machine, backlight and a blue tint. "Golem" moved to the Castello di Vogogna, between live footage and AI-generated story scenes, where all live play scenes are enshrouded in an acid green tint.

Part of the band footage comes from phones: Xiaomi 11T and 13T with BlackMagic Camera, manual exposure, no stabilization. Less convenient, more control.

Around the videos I handle the 2D side — graphics and thumbnails in Photoshop and Illustrator — and the vertical shorts that promote the band.

AI video

The first AI work was full Video Gen visualizers for Crownlake: generated footage, selection, editing, grade. Then "Golem" mixed live footage with AI-generated scenes, with the problem of making the two look like the same film.

The reference is not the style but the quality: a generation almost indistinguishable from the footage. The style, if anything, goes from a nihilistic digital surrealism to cyberpast and steampunk — territory I want to explore further.

The method is the same as the rest of the pipeline: generate more than needed, select, composite, grade. The effect should not be visible; the scene should just work.

Tools

DaVinci Resolve Studio
Primary. Editing, multicam, color and Fairlight. Studio license for ProRes and H.265 on Linux.
Premiere Pro
Fallback. Comfort zone and safety net: if Resolve is not available, the project does not stop.
After Effects
Effects. About half of the effects work. Fusion is growing, but not yet enough to leave AE.
Photoshop / Illustrator
2D. Graphics, thumbnails, mattes and title assets.
AI video tools
Generation. Visualizers and scenes, with selection and compositing in the pipeline.
GoPro · DJI · Xiaomi
Shooting. Hero 7 Black, Mini 2 and Xiaomi 11T/13T with BlackMagic Camera: MotoVlog, behind the scenes, live footage.

Formats and delivery

Resolution
4K shooting, 2K delivery.
Frame rate
25 fps for music videos; 60 fps for documentary-style footage such as MotoVlog.
Codecs
ProRes 422 HQ for archive and editing; H.264/H.265 for delivery.
Color
Rec.709 / sRGB, checked on a calibrated screen.
Audio
48 kHz, stereo, integrated loudness around −14 LUFS for web.
Package
Master, web version, vertical shorts, project files sorted by folder.
